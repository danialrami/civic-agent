# How It's Been Worked Out in the Past

Several solutions have emerged to help voters navigate elections. Each addresses part of the problem, but none provide a complete, personalized, push-based solution.

## Vote.org and Vote411.org

**What they do:**
- Provide registration status checks
- Show polling locations
- Offer ballot information for upcoming elections
- Send email reminders (if you remember to sign up)

**Limitations:**
- Pull-based: You must visit the website
- Generic: Not tailored to your specific address/races
- Limited candidate info: Often just names, not platforms or news

## Ballotpedia

**What they do:**
- Comprehensive encyclopedia of elections and candidates
- Detailed candidate biographies and policy positions
- Ballot measure explanations

**Limitations:**
- Information overload: Hard to find just your specific ballot
- Not personalized: You navigate through states/counties to find your races
- No calendar integration
- Requires active effort to check regularly

## State and County Election Websites

**What they do:**
- Official source for election dates, polling locations, and sample ballots
- Provide early voting schedules

**Limitations:**
- Poor UX: Many county websites are outdated and hard to navigate
- Inconsistent: Each county has a different system
- No candidate information beyond names
- No proactive notifications

## Political Party Scorecards

**What they do:**
- Rate candidates based on party alignment
- Provide voter guides for party members

**Limitations:**
- Partisan: Only cover one party's perspective
- Limited scope: Often ignore non-partisan local races
- Not helpful for independent or crossover voters

## Mailers and Sample Ballots

**What they do:**
- Arrive in mail with candidate statements
- Include polling location and hours

**Limitations:**
- Timing: Often arrive too close to election day
- Incomplete: May not cover all races or propositions
- Biased: Funded by campaigns, not neutral
- Not digital: Can't sync to calendar or search easily

## Social Media Reminders

**What they do:**
- Friends/shareholders post "go vote" reminders

**Limitations:**
- Infrequent and unreliable
- Rarely include specific ballot information
- No long-term calendar integration
